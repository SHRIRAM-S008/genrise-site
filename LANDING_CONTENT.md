## 2. The Problem We Exist to Solve

**Section title:** The internet is full of paywalls for simple things.

**Copy:**
Compressing a photo for a government exam form. Merging two PDFs. Making a passport-size photo. Generating an invoice.
These are everyday tasks, yet most websites that do them either:

- charge a subscription for a 10-second job,
- add a watermark until you pay,
- force you to create an account, or
- upload your private files to a server you know nothing about.

Students, job seekers, freelancers, and small businesses pay the price — in money, time, and privacy.

**Closing line:** We think that's backwards. So we're fixing it, one tool at a time.

## 3. Our Model — The Free-First Promise

**Section title:** Our Free-First Promise

**Copy:**
If a tool costs us nothing to run, it costs you nothing to use. That's the rule.

We design our products so they run directly in your browser — no servers processing your files, no cloud bills piling up. Because they're cheap for us to run, we can keep them free.

**Three pillars:**

1. **Free by default**
   If it doesn't cost us money to run, it stays free. No trials, no "pro" tier for basic features, no watermarks.

2. **Funded by services, not by you**
   Our client work — software development, SaaS builds, web design and digital marketing — pays for the free products. When a business hires us, it helps keep public tools free for everyone.

3. **Fair limits when it costs us**
   Some products need paid infrastructure — servers, AI compute, storage. For those, we offer a free plan with usage limits, and simple paid plans for people who need more. Light users stay free; heavy users help cover the running cost.

## 11. FAQ

**Is GenRise Tools really free?**
Yes. No sign-up, no watermarks, no limits. The tools run in your browser, so they cost us almost nothing to operate — and we keep it that way.

**How do you make money?**
Through our services — software development, SaaS builds, web design, security and digital marketing for businesses. That revenue funds our free products.

**Will you ever charge for tools?**
Not for tools that cost us nothing to run. If a future product needs paid servers or AI compute, it will have a free plan with usage limits, plus paid plans for people who need more.

**Are my files safe?**
Yes. GenRise Tools processes everything locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to our servers.

**Is your code open source?**
Many of our products are. GenRise Tools is available on GitHub, and anyone can contribute.

**I'm a beginner. Can I still contribute?**
Absolutely. Look for issues labelled `good first issue`, or help with docs, design or testing.

**Can I hire GenRise Tech for my business?**
Yes — use the contact form or WhatsApp. We'll reply within 24 hours.

**Do you work with clients outside India?**
Yes. We work with clients worldwide and collaborate remotely across time zones.

**I want project experience. Can I join a project?**
Yes. Our open-source products are open to everyone who wants real project experience. When we take on client work, we bring in freelancers from our contributor community alongside our core team.
